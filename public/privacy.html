PlayBox — Privacy Policy

PlayBox is a collection of casual games available as a Telegram Mini App and in the browser.

What we store

  • On your device (local storage): game progress, settings, language, high scores, crystals and boosters.
  • On our server (Supabase): an anonymous account ID, or your Telegram user ID if you play in Telegram or log in with Telegram; your nickname; high scores and points per game; bug reports you choose to send (text, optional screenshot, device and browser info).

We do not collect your name, phone number, email or location.

Ads and cookies

Ads are shown by third-party ad networks (for example Adsgram in Telegram and Google AdSense for Games on the web). If you choose “Accept all”, these partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to show and measure ads. With “Only necessary”, we ask them to show non-personalised ads. You can change your choice by clearing the site data.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Telegram (Stars) or by our payment provider on the web. We never see your card details.

Deleting your data

Use the bug report button (red triangle) and ask for deletion; we remove your account, nickname and scores.
